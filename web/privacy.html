STET · PRIVACY

Last updated 27 November 2026

STET is a free screenplay editor. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no advertising and no analytics. Your screenplays stay on your computer unless you choose one of the features below.

GOOGLE DRIVE SYNC

If you connect Google Drive, STET copies the screenplays you open and save to a folder called STET in your Drive, so you can reach them from other computers.

  • STET asks only for access to files it creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see or change anything else in your Drive.
  • Your screenplays go directly from your computer to Google. They never pass through anyone else.
  • Your sign-in is kept on your computer, encrypted by your operating system. It is never shared.
  • You can turn sync off for any screenplay, or disconnect Google Drive at any time from the File menu. Disconnecting gives the sign-in back to Google. Your files stay in your Drive and on your computer.

STET's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

REVISE

Revise is optional. When you start it, the parts of the screenplay you choose are sent to Anthropic's Claude, through your own Claude Code sign-in or your own API key, to suggest corrections. STET shows you what will be sent before anything goes.

OTHER CONNECTIONS

  • Updates: STET checks GitHub for a new version. Nothing about you or your screenplays is sent.
  • Dictionaries: when you add a spelling language, STET downloads it from public dictionary sources.

THE BROWSER VERSION

The browser version keeps drafts in your browser's own storage. If you connect Google Drive there, the same rules as above apply.
